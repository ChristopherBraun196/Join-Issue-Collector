Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Join ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and handles personal information when you use Join, a Kanban project management tool with an issue collector. Join is a demo project created for educational purposes at the Developer Akademie and is not intended for commercial use.

1. Information We Collect

When you use Join, we process the following information:

Account Information: If you sign up, we store your name, your email address and your login. Your password is handled by Firebase Authentication and is never visible to us.
Guest Login: If you log in as a guest, an anonymous account without name or email is created.
Contacts: Names, email addresses, phone numbers and optional profile photos of the contacts you create, including your own account.
Tasks: Titles, descriptions, due dates, priorities, categories, subtasks, assigned contacts and the name of the creator.
Attachments: Images you add to tasks (JPEG or PNG), stored together with their file name, type and size. Images are compressed in your browser before they are saved.
Requests via the Issue Collector: If you send a request by email or through the stakeholder form, we store your name, your email address and the content of your request.
Technical Data: When you visit the website, the web server automatically records technical data such as your IP address, browser type and the time of access in server logs.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect solely to:

+ Provide the board, task, contact and file upload functionality
+ Log you in and keep your data secure
+ Turn requests from the issue collector into tasks on the board
+ Notify the sender of a request by email when the status of their task changes
+ Allow team members to reply to the sender of a request by email
+ Fix technical issues and prevent abuse

3. Service Providers

We use the following services to run Join:

Firebase (Google): Firebase Authentication handles the login and the Firebase Realtime Database stores all tasks, contacts and attachments. The database is located in the EU (europe-west1).
n8n on our own server at netcup: Emails sent to the issue collector are processed by an n8n workflow that we host ourselves on a server of netcup GmbH in Germany.
Google Gemini: To turn an incoming email into a task, its content is sent to Google Gemini, which creates the title, description and subtasks. Such tasks are marked as "Ai-generated".
Hosting: The website itself is hosted by ALL-INKL.COM - Neue Medien Münnich in Germany.

4. Data Storage and Security

Access to the data requires a login, and the database only accepts data in the expected format. We take reasonable technical measures to protect your data, but no transmission over the internet is 100% secure. As this is a demo project, please do not store sensitive personal information or private images in Join.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

We keep your data as long as your account exists. You can delete your account yourself at any time under "My account" → "Delete my account". This removes your contact, your login and your assignment to all tasks. Tasks, contacts and attachments can be deleted directly in the application. You can also ask us to delete your data by email.

6. Sharing of Information

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information. Apart from the service providers listed above, we only share data if required by law.

7. Local Storage

Join does not use tracking or advertising cookies. Firebase stores your login session in your browser so that you stay logged in. You can remove it at any time by logging out or clearing your browser data.

8. Your Rights

Under the GDPR you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
Rectification: Correct inaccurate data, most of it directly under "My account".
Erasure: Delete your account and data as described above.
Portability: Request your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
Objection: Object to certain types of data processing.
Complaint: Lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.

9. Children's Privacy

Join is not intended for children under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Join changes. The date at the top shows the last update.

11. Disclaimer

Join is a demo/educational project and is not intended for production use. This privacy policy is provided for demonstration purposes only and does not constitute legal advice.
